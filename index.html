The Creative Sparks Behind the Code.

Every project in this portfolio began with a simple question: what if we borrowed proven principles from physics, geometry, and psychology to solve fundamental computer science challenges?

Act I • The Celestial Sphere

Astrophysical Distance Measurements

How do astronomers gauge distance across billions of light years? They separate intrinsic stellar brightness from celestial direction. Modern AI embeddings mix vector length and angle together, requiring massive RAM. By separating them cleanly, Project Milky Way shrinks AI model memory by 16x without losing semantic accuracy.

Project Milky Way → 16x Memory Reduction
Act II • Combat Psychology

Defeating Exploit Loops Without Cheating

In video games, boss enemies often feel cheap because they either have massive health bars or read the player's controller buttons in 10 milliseconds. In reality, human players rely on repetitive habits like a to b to c to a. In Weatherer, the AI observes your pattern, predicts your next move, and evolves counter-strategies naturally.

Weatherer → Organic Adaptive AI
Act III • The Complex Plane

Separating Trends from Noise in \(\mathbb{C}\)

When deep neural networks forecast volatile data like financial markets or sensor telemetry, sudden spikes corrupt their predictions. In CV-OSNS, we treat data as complex numbers (\(a + ib\)): the real number tracks the genuine underlying trend, while the imaginary number absorbs the erratic volatility spikes.

CV-OSNS → Noise-Resistant Forecasting

Project Milky Way: High-Efficiency Embedding Compression via Spherical Decomposition

The Creative Genesis:

Inspired by astronomical standard candles (measuring star distance separately from direction), this codec decouples dense AI vector embeddings into a single radial scale factor and a compact directional bit-vector.

Shrinks massive text and search embeddings down to a tiny fraction of their original memory footprint with minimal loss in search accuracy. Proves that vector orientation preserves semantic meaning while radial distance captures intensity.

High compression ratio: reduces RAM usage by over 90%

📐 Complex Numbers in Machine Learning Time-Series • Uncertainty

CV-OSNS: Complex-Valued Signal Separation for Volatile Time-Series

The Creative Genesis:

By representing neural predictions as complex numbers (\(a + ib\)), the network acts as a "gradient traffic cop" that directs smooth deterministic trends to the real component and sudden volatility shocks to the imaginary component.

Designed for high-volatility environments like financial markets and industrial sensor streams where standard neural nets suffer catastrophic gradient breakdown during sudden regime changes and price shocks.

Orthogonal loss design preventing noise from polluting core predictions
Comprehensive test battery verifying stability under market regime shifts
Dual-network deep architecture with explicit uncertainty bounds
